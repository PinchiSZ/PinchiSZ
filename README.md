### 👋 About me

I'm an **Information Systems student** focused on **software development, algorithms, databases, cloud technologies, and cybersecurity**.

I'm currently developing my skills across the stack, with a particular interest in **front-end development, professional design, backend systems, and secure software architecture**.

I've recently completed an intensive **Java + Spring Boot AI bootcamp**, where I improved two robust projects that are now public here.

🎯 My long-term goal is to become a **fullstack developer**, while gradually specializing in **cybersecurity**.

🔐 **Cybersecurity is the field I ultimately want to build my career around.** I'm especially interested in offensive security, penetration testing, secure development, cloud security, and understanding how systems can be attacked — and, more importantly, how they can be built to resist those attacks.

☁️ I'm also exploring **Azure, AWS, Docker, and DevOps practices**, with plans to pursue cloud and technology certifications and potentially work with DevOps or cloud infrastructure along the way.

♿ I'm interested in **accessibility, sustainability, Green IT, and Design Justice** because I believe technology should not only work — it should be **inclusive, responsible, efficient, and designed with real people in mind**.

🌱 I'm particularly interested in **Green IT and sustainable software practices**, including reducing unnecessary resource consumption and thinking about the environmental impact of the systems we build.

🎮 A gamer at heart and endlessly curious about **technology, biology, geography, and the future**. I'd love to create software that connects technology with these fields to produce meaningful innovation.

The people affected by technology should have a voice in how that technology is designed.
