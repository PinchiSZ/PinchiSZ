## 👋 About me

I'm an **Information Systems student** focused on **software development, algorithms, databases, cloud technologies, and cybersecurity**.

I'm currently building my skills across the stack, with a particular interest in **front-end development, backend systems, professional design, cloud infrastructure, and secure software architecture**.

I've recently completed an intensive **Java + Spring Boot AI bootcamp**, where I improved two robust projects that are now public here.

🎯 My long-term goal is to become a **fullstack developer**, while gradually specializing in **cybersecurity**.

🔐 **Cybersecurity is the field I ultimately want to build my career around.** I'm particularly interested in penetration testing, ethical hacking, application security, cloud security, network security, and secure coding.

☁️ I'm also exploring **Azure, AWS, Docker, and DevOps practices**, with plans to pursue cloud certifications and gain practical experience with modern infrastructure.

♿ I believe in **accessibility, Design Justice, sustainability, and Green IT**, because technology should be more than functional, it should be **inclusive, responsible, efficient, and designed with real people in mind**.

🌱 I'm particularly interested in **Green IT and sustainable software**, including reducing unnecessary resource consumption and considering the environmental impact of the systems we build.

🎮 A gamer at heart and endlessly curious about **technology, biology, geography, and the future**.

# 🌱 Technology with Purpose

I believe good technology should consider more than functionality.

### ♿ Accessibility

Software should be usable by as many people as possible, regardless of their abilities or circumstances.

### ⚖️ Design Justice

The people affected by technology should have a voice in how that technology is designed.

### 🌱 Green IT

Using technology wisely will ensure computer processes/environmental damage are minimized.

### ♻️ Sustainability

New technology should help provide long-term solutions, not increase production and consumption.

For me, these aren't separate concepts from software engineering — they're part of **building better technology**.
